# Bill of Materials

## Mechanical

Most of the mechanical stuff is the 3d prints. no special printing needed. When assembling, make sure there is clearance to plug in the usbc cable. If stuff isn't aligned, you wont be able to plug it in. screws thread directly into print. be careful not to over torque and strip the hole of anything it would have that the screw could hold onto.
